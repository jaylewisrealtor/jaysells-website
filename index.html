Frequently Asked Questions

Do you help both buyers and sellers in the Edmonton area?

Yes. Jay works with both home buyers and sellers across Edmonton and surrounding communities, providing local market guidance, pricing strategy, and negotiation support for both sides of a transaction.

What areas does Jay Lewis serve?

Edmonton, Beaumont, Devon, Fort Saskatchewan, Leduc, Morinville, Onoway, Sherwood Park, Spruce Grove, St. Albert, and Stony Plain.

How much does it cost to use a realtor when buying a home?

In most cases in Alberta, the seller pays commission for both the listing agent and the buyer's agent, so there is typically no direct cost to you as a buyer to be represented.

What is the first step to selling my home?

A free, no-obligation home evaluation and market analysis, based on recent comparable sales in your specific neighborhood — before you commit to anything.
