About Alexandra Park

Alexandra Park is a centrally located, affordable neighbourhood in the heart of Leduc, just off 50th Street and adjacent to the Leduc Civic Centre. Alexandra Park — the park itself — features sports fields, a spray park, playgrounds and walking paths, with the Leduc Recreation Centre and Aquatic Centre just steps away.

Residents are within walking distance of Leduc's downtown core, with independent cafes and dining options along 50th Avenue. Families are served by the Black Gold School Division, with East Elementary School serving younger children and convenient access to Leduc Junior High School and Leduc Composite High School.
