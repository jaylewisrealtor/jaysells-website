About Leduc

Leduc is a city of nearly 40,000 people immediately south of Edmonton, home to Edmonton International Airport and the Nisku Industrial Business Park. The city traces its modern identity to the historic Leduc No. 1 oil discovery of February 1947, which launched Alberta's oil boom and put the region on the map. Leduc has grown by more than 17% over the past five years.

Central neighbourhoods like Alexandra Park — home to the Alexandra Outdoor Pool and Spray Park, next to the Leduc Civic Centre and Recreation Centre — sit within walking distance of the city's downtown core along 50th Avenue. Other notable neighbourhoods include Southfork, Meadowview Park and Corinthia Park.
