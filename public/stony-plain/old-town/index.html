About Old Town

Old Town sits right in the heart of downtown Stony Plain, an established neighbourhood of charming cottages and bungalow-style homes. Residents are just steps from Stony Plain's shops, restaurants and cafes, while still enjoying a quiet residential feel.

Old Town's character homes date back decades, so evaluating true construction condition matters more here than comparing new-build specs.
