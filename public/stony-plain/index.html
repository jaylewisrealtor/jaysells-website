About Stony Plain

Stony Plain is a town of nearly 20,000 people, located west of Edmonton and adjacent to the City of Spruce Grove, within the Edmonton Metropolitan Region. Known as "the Town with the Painted Past," Stony Plain is home to more than 36 life-sized murals depicting the area's pioneer history, along with the Multicultural Heritage Centre — a living museum built around the 1925 Old Brick School and the 1910 Oppertshauser House, and home to the only public art gallery located in rural Alberta.

Residents share access to the TransAlta Tri Leisure Centre with neighbouring Spruce Grove and Parkland County, along with three major parks and an interconnected trail system.

Stony Plain is made up of distinct neighbourhoods — including Old Town, Graybriar, The Fairways and Rosenthal Estates — ranging from character homes downtown to golf-course and luxury acreage-style communities.
