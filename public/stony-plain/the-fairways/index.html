About The Fairways

The Fairways is a residential community that began development in the mid-1990s, sitting adjacent to the Stony Plain Golf Course. Homeowners enjoy golf course views, walking paths and ravines, along with Fairway Park, a playground and green space for recreation.

The community is mostly detached single-family homes, with an exclusive gated condominium option for buyers who prefer minimal yard work. Residents have easy access to both Highway 16A and Highway 628, making commuting straightforward.

Buying or Selling in The Fairways

The Fairways has been building since the mid-1990s, so home condition and updates vary across the community.
