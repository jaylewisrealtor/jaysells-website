About Graybriar

Graybriar backs directly onto the Stony Plain Golf Course and is home to some of the most sought-after townhouses in Stony Plain, alongside large detached houses on spacious lots and adult-living condo options. The community includes a network of walking and jogging paths, common green spaces, and sits next to Atim Creek.

Located on the east side of Stony Plain, Graybriar offers convenient access to Edmonton via Highway 628 or Highway 16A, making it a practical choice for commuters.

Buying or Selling in Graybriar
