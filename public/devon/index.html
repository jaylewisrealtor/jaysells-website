About Devon

Devon is a town of nearly 7,000 people on the North Saskatchewan River, about 26 kilometres southwest of Edmonton. It was built by Imperial Oil following the historic Leduc No. 1 oil discovery of February 1947, and holds the distinction of being the first Canadian community approved by a regional planning commission.

Devon's identity is closely tied to its river valley setting, with 24 kilometres of trails connecting Voyageur Park to Parkland County, the Devon Golf & Conference Centre, a mountain bike skills park, and the well-known Legs of Fire Stairs.
