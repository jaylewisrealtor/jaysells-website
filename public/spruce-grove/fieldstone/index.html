About Fieldstone

Fieldstone is a centrally located Spruce Grove neighbourhood, north of Grove Drive and east of Calahoo Road, that began development in the late 1980s. Many properties back directly onto The Links golf course, and the community features an array of custom-built homes with mature trees throughout.

Fieldstone is directly across the road from Spruce Grove Composite High School, making it a practical option for families as well as buyers drawn to its golf-course setting.

Buying or Selling in Fieldstone

As an established community dating to the late 1980s, many Fieldstone homes have since been updated or renovated, so construction quality varies significantly by property. With more than 20 years of residential construction experience, Jay Lewis helps buyers evaluate what's actually been updated versus cosmetically refreshed, and helps sellers price accordingly.
