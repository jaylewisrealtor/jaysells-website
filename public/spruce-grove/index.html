About Spruce Grove

Spruce Grove is a city of more than 44,000 people, located about 11 kilometres west of Edmonton and adjacent to the Town of Stony Plain, surrounded by Parkland County. It has been one of the fastest-growing cities in Alberta over the past decade, with population growth averaging more than 3% a year.

The city is home to the TransAlta Tri Leisure Centre, a large recreation facility shared with Stony Plain and Parkland County, along with the Horizon Stage Performing Arts Centre and more than 40 kilometres of trails, including the Heritage Grove Trail through mature forest.

Spruce Grove is made up of many distinct neighbourhoods — including Aspen Trails, Jesperdale, Deer Park, Prescott and Fieldstone — ranging from established, golf-course-adjacent communities to active new-construction areas.
