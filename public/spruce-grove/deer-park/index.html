About Deer Park

Deer Park is a community in the heart of Spruce Grove, with construction that began in the early 1990s and continues today. Homes typically feature two levels of living space, front-loading garages, three to five bedrooms and 1,500 to 2,500 square feet, with larger detached homes offering four bedrooms and 2,000+ square feet.

Deer Park is bordered by major commuting routes including Jennifer Heil Way, Grove Drive and Highway 16, and is just minutes from the TransAlta Tri Leisure Centre.

New Construction and Resale in Deer Park

Because Deer Park has been building since the early 1990s and continues to develop today, it includes both established resale homes and new construction side by side. With more than 20 years of residential construction experience, Jay Lewis helps buyers compare the two directly on real construction quality.
