About Grandin

Grandin sits on the south bank of the Sturgeon River and is one of St. Albert's oldest and largest neighbourhoods, home to about 7,300 residents. The neighbourhood is rich in green space, with parks including Grosvenor, Greenbrier, Grenadier, Glenwood, Gatewood, Grandin Ravine and Grandin Pond EcoPark offering river valley views throughout.

Grosvenor Park is home to St. Albert's only outdoor swimming pool, the Grosvenor Outdoor Pool, a distinctive feature for the neighbourhood.

Buying or Selling in Grandin

As one of St. Albert's oldest neighbourhoods, most homes in Grandin were built decades ago, so construction age and condition vary meaningfully from property to property.
