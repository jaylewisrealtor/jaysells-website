About Jensen Lakes

Jensen Lakes is St. Albert's first and only beach community, built around a man-made lake with a private beach club offering sandy beaches, swimming, kayaking and paddleboarding. It's one of the newest neighbourhoods in St. Albert, with modern homes featuring contemporary designs, spacious yards, large windows and open-concept layouts.

The lakefront setting and resort-style amenities make Jensen Lakes distinct from St. Albert's more established, mature neighbourhoods, appealing to buyers looking for new construction with a recreational lifestyle built in.

New Construction in Jensen Lakes

Jensen Lakes is one of St. Albert's most active new-construction communities, with ongoing development around the lake. With more than 20 years of residential construction experience, Jay Lewis helps buyers evaluate builders, lot location and specifications in a fast-developing community like Jensen Lakes.
