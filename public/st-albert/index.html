About St. Albert

St. Albert is one of Alberta's oldest communities, founded in 1861 as a Métis and French-Canadian settlement and recognized as the oldest non-fortified community in the province. It was incorporated as a city in 1977 and today has a population of more than 74,000, immediately northwest of Edmonton.

The city's history is preserved at sites including St. Albert Grain Elevator Park, home to two provincially designated historic grain elevators, and River Lot 23 & 24, where some of the oldest surviving houses in the area tell the story of the original French-Canadian and Métis settlers. St. Albert Botanic Park and the Art Gallery of St. Albert round out a community known for its established, family-oriented neighbourhoods and consistently high resident satisfaction ratings.

St. Albert is made up of many distinct neighbourhoods — including Erin Ridge, Jensen Lakes, Riverside, Lacombe Park and Grandin — each with its own character, from lakefront new construction to established, river-valley living.

Buying or Selling in St. Albert

St. Albert is largely a built-out, established community, with most of its housing stock in mature neighbourhoods rather than new subdivisions. With more than 20 years of residential construction experience, Jay Lewis helps buyers evaluate the condition, age and construction quality of resale homes — important in a city where much of the inventory was built decades ago — alongside the newer developments still underway at the city's edges.

For sellers, that means pricing based on real comparable sales in a specific, well-established neighbourhood, not a citywide average.

St. Albert FAQ

How far is St. Albert from Edmonton?

St. Albert is immediately northwest of Edmonton, bordering the city, and is part of the Edmonton Metropolitan Region.

Is St. Albert a good place to raise a family?

St. Albert is known for established, family-oriented neighbourhoods, mature parks and heritage sites, and the city's own Community Satisfaction Survey has reported a 99% positive rating on quality of life from residents.

What is St. Albert known for?

St. Albert is known for being one of Alberta's oldest communities, founded in 1861, and for heritage sites including St. Albert Grain Elevator Park, home to two provincially designated historic grain elevators, and St. Albert Botanic Park.

What neighbourhoods are in St. Albert?

Notable neighbourhoods include Erin Ridge, Jensen Lakes, Riverside, Lacombe Park and Grandin, among others.
