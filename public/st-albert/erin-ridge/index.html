About Erin Ridge

Erin Ridge is a St. Albert neighbourhood of about 5,960 residents, offering a mix of low- and medium-density housing with scenic views. The community has interconnected trails, a basketball court, baseball diamonds, soccer fields and play structures throughout its parks.

Erin Ridge is close to major amenities including Costco, Walmart and Sturgeon Community Hospital, making it a practical choice for buyers who want convenience alongside residential quiet. Home prices in the area have historically ranged from the $300s to the high $600s, reflecting a mix of housing types.
