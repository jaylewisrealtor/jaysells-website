About Lacombe Park

Lacombe Park is the largest neighbourhood in St. Albert, home to about 7,500 residents with a median age of 42. It offers a mix of housing styles — single-family homes, townhouses, duplexes and condos — along with parks, green spaces, schools and commercial services. Most residents live in single-detached homes, with average prices in the $500,000s.

St. Albert Transit (StAT) serves Lacombe Park with weekday local routes, commuter routes to Edmonton, and Dial-A-Bus service, making it a practical choice for commuters as well as families.
