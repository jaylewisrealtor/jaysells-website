About Riverside

Riverside is situated along the Sturgeon River, with many properties offering picturesque river views. The neighbourhood is well suited to buyers who want proximity to river valley trails and lakes while staying close to urban conveniences.

Riverside connects to Edmonton via Ray Gibbon Drive, making it a practical choice for commuters who also want a quieter, nature-oriented setting at home.

Buying or Selling in Riverside

Riverside's river valley setting is a genuine differentiator, and lot position relative to the river and trail system can meaningfully affect a property's value. With more than 20 years of residential construction experience, Jay Lewis helps buyers and sellers understand what that positioning is actually worth, alongside new-construction options for buyers considering both.
