Frequently Asked Questions

Should I buy new construction or resale in Edmonton?

Both have trade-offs. New construction offers newer systems, warranties and customization, but requires understanding builder reputations, specifications and contracts. Resale homes offer established neighbourhoods and known pricing history, but may need updates. Jay's residential construction background helps buyers evaluate both options based on the actual condition and value of a property, not just its age.

Do I need a REALTOR® when buying from an Edmonton home builder?

Builders' sales representatives work for the builder, not the buyer. A buyer's REALTOR® reviews contracts, upgrade pricing, lot selection and walkthrough details on the buyer's behalf. Compensation and representation terms should be discussed and documented clearly before a buyer enters into an agreement with a builder.

What should I look for when choosing an Edmonton home builder?

Key factors include the builder's track record on similar homes, warranty coverage, standard specifications versus upgrades, construction timelines, and how deficiencies are handled after possession. Jay's background in residential construction is used to review these details with buyers before they commit.

What should I check during a new-home walkthrough?

A pre-possession walkthrough should cover finishes, fixtures, appliances, doors and windows, paint, flooring, and confirmation that upgrades match the contract. Noting deficiencies in writing before possession makes it easier to have them addressed by the builder.

What areas around Edmonton have new construction?

New construction is active in communities including Edmonton, Beaumont, Leduc, Spruce Grove, Stony Plain and Fort Saskatchewan, among others. Availability changes as communities develop, so current options are best confirmed directly.

How does the first-time home buyer GST rebate work on a new home?

The GST new housing rebate can reduce the amount of GST paid on a new home, and eligibility and rebate amounts depend on the purchase price, intended use of the property, and current federal program rules, which have changed in recent years. Because these rules affect every situation differently, buyers should confirm current GST rebate details with their builder, lawyer or a tax professional before relying on a specific number.

How much does it cost to sell a home in Edmonton?

Selling costs typically include REALTOR® commission, legal fees, and any costs related to preparing the home for sale. Commission is negotiated between the seller and their brokerage and varies by situation, so exact costs should be discussed directly before listing.
