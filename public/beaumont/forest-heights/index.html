About Forest Heights

Forest Heights sits on the eastern outskirts of Beaumont, known for its scenic beauty and average-to-above-average home and lot sizes. The neighbourhood offers sizable single-family homes and duplexes on large executive and estate lots, tucked away from the busier parts of town, with some smaller, more affordable homes also available.

Buying or Selling in Forest Heights

Forest Heights' larger executive and estate lots command a premium, so getting the construction quality assessment right matters. With more than 20 years of residential construction experience, Jay Lewis helps buyers evaluate what a Forest Heights home's size and lot actually justify in price, and helps sellers present an estate-lot property to its full value.
