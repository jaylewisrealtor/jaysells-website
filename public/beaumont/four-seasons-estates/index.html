About Four Seasons Estates

Four Seasons Estates is a smaller housing development on the southwest side of Beaumont, with a quiet, serene setting near Don Sparrow Lake and several local parks. Homes typically have a traditional look with front-loading garages and three to four bedrooms, alongside some sizable luxury properties.

A trail system and sidewalks line every road, and Montrose Village, a shopping area with a pet clinic, liquor store and local restaurants, sits just south of the neighbourhood.
