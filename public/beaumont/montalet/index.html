About Montalet

Montalet is a 127-acre community built by Anthem between 2003 and 2013, drawing on Beaumont's origins as a French farming community with distinctive French-inspired architecture, tree-lined boulevards and two ponds. The community is primarily traditional single-family and estate homes, with more affordable duplexes also available.

Buying or Selling in Montalet

Montalet was built out over a decade, so home age within the community ranges from newer to established. With more than 20 years of residential construction experience, Jay Lewis helps buyers evaluate a Montalet home's actual construction era and quality, and helps sellers price accordingly.
