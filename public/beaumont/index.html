About Beaumont

Beaumont is one of the fastest-growing municipalities in Alberta, with a population that has grown from about 7,300 in 2001 to more than 26,000 today — an increase of roughly 239%. Located just minutes south of Edmonton, Beaumont is also Alberta's largest officially bilingual municipality, reflecting its history as a French farming community, and is projected to reach 30,000 residents by 2030.

Four Seasons Park, a 51-acre park with a trout-stocked fishing pond, sports fields and a spray park, anchors the community alongside neighbourhoods including Four Seasons Estates, Montalet, Forest Heights and Élan.
