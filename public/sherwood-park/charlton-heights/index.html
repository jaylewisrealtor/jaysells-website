About Charlton Heights

Charlton Heights is a well-established Sherwood Park neighbourhood, with most homes built between 1990 and the early 2000s, ranging from single-family houses to townhouses. Mature trees, parks and green spaces give the community a settled, established feel.

Emerald Hills Park provides playgrounds, picnic areas and sports fields, and the nearby Strathcona Athletic Park adds facilities for soccer, baseball and other sports. Indoor recreation is available close by at Millennium Place and the Emerald Hills Leisure Centre. Families have access to schools including Mills Haven Elementary and Clover Bar Junior High, and the community is just minutes from Sherwood Park's downtown shops, restaurants and cafes.
