About Sherwood Park

Sherwood Park is a hamlet within Strathcona County, immediately east of Edmonton, with a population of more than 75,000 — making it the largest hamlet in the world according to Guinness World Records. It was built starting in the mid-1950s as a residential community for workers at Edmonton's Refinery Row, and has grown into an established area known for its schools, recreation facilities, parks and trail system.

Because Sherwood Park is a hamlet rather than an incorporated city, it's governed by Strathcona County, which had a population of more than 107,000 in 2025. Strathcona County includes both the urban area of Sherwood Park and surrounding rural areas.

Local landmarks include Festival Place, a performing arts and entertainment venue that hosts concerts and events year-round, along with annual events such as the Strathcona County Fair and the Strathcona County Farmers' Market. Sherwood Park is also made up of many distinct neighbourhoods — including Summerwood, Cambrian, Lakeland Ridge, Foxboro and Charlton Heights — each with its own character.

Sherwood Park FAQ

Is Sherwood Park part of Edmonton?

No. Sherwood Park is a hamlet within Strathcona County, a separate municipality immediately east of Edmonton. It is not part of the City of Edmonton, though it is part of the Edmonton Metropolitan Region.

What is Sherwood Park known for?

Sherwood Park is known for its schools, parks, recreation facilities and trail system, and for being the largest hamlet in the world according to Guinness World Records, with a population of more than 75,000.

What neighbourhoods are in Sherwood Park?

Sherwood Park is made up of many distinct neighbourhoods, including Summerwood, Cambrian, Lakeland Ridge, Foxboro and Charlton Heights, among others.
