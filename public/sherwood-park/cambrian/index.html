About Cambrian

Cambrian is Sherwood Park's newest master-planned community, spanning about 310 acres along Old Man Creek. It's designed with 7 parks, 4 ponds, 2 playgrounds and 8 kilometres of multi-use trails, with future plans for commercial sites, a school, and multi-family housing alongside single-family homes.

The community offers a range of housing types, from ravine-backing estate homes to townhomes and duplexes, built by several builders including ART Homes, Bedrock Homes, Gill Built Homes, Rohit Communities, San Rufo Homes and Victory Homes. Cambrian is about 4 minutes from Emerald Hills Shopping Centre, Emerald Hills Leisure Centre, Strathcona Community Hospital and Archbishop Jordan High School.
