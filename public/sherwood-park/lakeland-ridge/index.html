About Lakeland Ridge

Lakeland Ridge is a newer, family-oriented community in northern Sherwood Park, built around a central lake with viewing docks and lake-view homes. Housing ranges from modern townhouses suited to first-time buyers or empty nesters to larger single-family homes with big yards for growing families.

Chatwin Park is the community's primary park, with a soccer field and playground connected to the rest of the neighbourhood by trails. The Trillium Centre is home to Rotary Park and the community's recreation centre, along with two school campuses. The Lakeland Ridge Shopping Centre, anchored by a Sobeys, provides everyday shopping and dining close to home.

New Construction and Resale in Lakeland Ridge

Lakeland Ridge's homes are mostly recent construction, with new-build activity still ongoing in parts of the community. With more than 20 years of residential construction experience, Jay Lewis helps buyers evaluate new builds in Lakeland Ridge against resale homes elsewhere in Sherwood Park.

Lakeland Ridge FAQ

What is Lakeland Ridge known for?

Lakeland Ridge is known for the lake at its centre, with viewing docks and lake-view homes, along with Chatwin Park and the Trillium Centre recreation and school campus.

What shopping is available in Lakeland Ridge?

The Lakeland Ridge Shopping Centre, built around a Sobeys grocery store, provides restaurants, a drug store, a dental office and other everyday amenities.
