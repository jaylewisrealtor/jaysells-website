About Foxboro

Foxboro is a mature neighbourhood on the east side of Sherwood Park, bordered by Baseline Road and Clover Bar Road. Homes are primarily large, two-storey, single-family houses well suited to growing families, alongside some townhouses.

Foxboro Park, the community's main green space, offers a playground, picnic area, sports field and pond, and the neighbourhood connects to Sherwood Park's Parkway Trail System, linking Foxboro to the neighbouring community of Heritage Hills. Families are served by nearby schools including Wes Hosford Elementary School and Bev Facey Community High School.

Buying or Selling in Foxboro

Foxboro's housing stock is well established, so evaluating construction age and condition matters more here than comparing new-build specifications. With more than 20 years of residential construction experience, Jay Lewis helps buyers assess a Foxboro home's condition and value against real comparable sales, and helps sellers price and prepare accordingly.
