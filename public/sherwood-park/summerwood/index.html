About Summerwood

Summerwood is a creekside community in Sherwood Park built around Old Man Creek, with landscaped parks, tree-lined trails and storm ponds woven throughout. It's consistently ranked among Sherwood Park's most family-friendly neighbourhoods, with public and Catholic schools serving local families.

Amenities include Sunfield Park, five playgrounds, a basketball court, community gardens, a winter skating surface and the Summerton Spray Deck splash park. Residents are close to the Emerald Hills Centre and Sherwood Park Mall for shopping, and Strathcona Community Hospital is less than 3 kilometres away.

Buying or Selling in Summerwood

Summerwood includes both established sections and newer construction, giving buyers a genuine choice between a resale home and a new build within the same community. With more than 20 years of residential construction experience, Jay Lewis helps buyers evaluate both against each other on real construction quality, not just price.
