Talk to Jay About New Construction

Why New Construction Needs Different Representation

New construction requires a different kind of representation than a resale purchase. With more than 20 years of residential construction experience, Jay Lewis helps buyers evaluate builders, communities, lots, specifications, upgrades, contracts, walkthroughs and deficiencies — not simply find a house.

Whether you're considering a quick-possession home, building from the ground up, or comparing a new home with resale, the goal is to understand exactly what you're buying before you commit.

What a Construction Background Adds

Builders' sales representatives work for the builder, not the buyer. Having a REALTOR® with hands-on residential construction experience — framing, renovations and complete contract builds — means someone is reviewing specifications, upgrade pricing, lot selection, contract terms and pre-possession walkthroughs on your behalf, with a working knowledge of how homes are actually built.

  • Evaluating builder reputation, warranty coverage and standard specifications
  • Reviewing contracts, upgrade pricing and lot selection
  • Comparing quick-possession homes, build-from-the-ground-up options and resale
  • Pre-possession walkthroughs, documenting deficiencies before they're missed
