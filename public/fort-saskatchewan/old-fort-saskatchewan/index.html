About Old Fort Saskatchewan

Old Fort Saskatchewan is the original heart of the city, offering character homes on mature lots, walkable streets, and proximity to the North Saskatchewan River valley trails. It's close to the Fort Heritage Precinct, which preserves the city's North West Mounted Police and provincial gaol history along the riverbank.

Buying or Selling in Old Fort Saskatchewan

Character homes in Old Fort Saskatchewan vary widely in age and condition, so a careful evaluation matters more here than in a newer subdivision. With more than 20 years of residential construction experience, Jay Lewis helps buyers assess a home's real construction quality in this historic core, and helps sellers understand what genuinely adds value.
