About Fort Saskatchewan

Fort Saskatchewan is a city of more than 30,000 people, located along the North Saskatchewan River about 25 kilometres northeast of Edmonton. It has been a city since 1967 and is part of the Edmonton Metropolitan Region, with population growth of more than 11% over the past five years.

The city's downtown Fort Heritage Precinct preserves its history along the riverbank, including the story of the North West Mounted Police and Alberta's provincial gaol. The Dow Centennial Centre, a 170,000-square-foot recreation and cultural facility built for the city's 2003–04 centennial, anchors community life alongside neighbourhoods such as Westpark, Pineview, Sherridon Heights, and the original downtown core, Old Fort Saskatchewan.

Fort Saskatchewan is about 25 kilometres northeast of Edmonton, along the North Saskatchewan River.

Notable neighbourhoods include Westpark, Pineview, Sherridon Heights and Old Fort Saskatchewan, the city's original downtown core.
