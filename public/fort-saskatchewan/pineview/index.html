About Pineview

Pineview is Fort Saskatchewan's quintessential established family neighbourhood, developed primarily in the 1970s and 1980s. It's defined by solid bungalows, wide curving streets, mature landscaping and an established tree canopy, with green spaces and pocket parks throughout, located just southwest of the intersection of Highways 15 and 21.

Carscadden/RCMP Park is the neighbourhood's largest park, offering green space, picnic areas, sports fields and an outdoor area. Pineview draws first-time buyers looking for solid homes to update, families who want to be within walking distance of schools, and investors drawn to the large lots.

Buying or Selling in Pineview

Pineview's 1970s and 1980s bungalows offer good lot sizes, but construction age means condition varies considerably between homes.
