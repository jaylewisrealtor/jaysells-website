About Westpark

Westpark sits along the North Saskatchewan River on the southwest side of Fort Saskatchewan. Students are served by Elk Island Public Schools and Elk Island Catholic Schools, including SouthPointe School, Win Ferguson Elementary, Fort Saskatchewan High School, St. John Paul II Catholic School and St. André Bessette Catholic High School.

The neighbourhood includes a wetland conservation area, a community fishpond, an interpretive forest, an off-leash dog area and several parks, with paved trails connecting directly to the river valley. A commercial district in the southeast corner offers restaurants, grocers and services.
